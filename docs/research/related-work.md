# Related-work log

The reading log behind §2 of [PAPER.md](PAPER.md). RP-03 starts it; RP-37 turns
it into prose once it holds 25+ entries. Every citation in §2 must be here first.

**Who fills what.** The citation line is checked against Crossref and arXiv.
Everything below it is written by the owner, in their own words, after reading
the paper. A blank field means *not read yet*, not *nothing to say*.

**Fields**

- **Question** — what the paper asks, in one sentence.
- **Data** — what it was measured on, and who authored that data.
- **Baselines** — what it compares against.
- **Metrics** — what it reports, and at what n.
- **Does not claim** — the limits the paper states, or the ones it should have.
- **How Sati differs** — specifically, not "we are grounded".
- **My takeaway** — one or two sentences.
